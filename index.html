ABOUT US

Making digital services simple.

ONE CLICK COMPUTERS is a digital service and computer center serving students, individuals, businesses and organizations in Kano State.

We provide practical computer, printing, design and online service assistance with a focus on convenience, quality and customer satisfaction. From a simple photocopy to a professional flyer or an online registration process, our goal is to make the task easier for you.
